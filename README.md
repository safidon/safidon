### ◆ Now building — [ERKEK AI](https://github.com/safidon/ERKEK-AI)

An AI-powered digital mentor for men: it helps think through work, money,
family and goals calmly, take responsibility and choose the next right step.
Speaks **Kazakh** and **Russian**.

- 💬 Streaming chat with long-term memory and conversation summaries
- 🎭 Three mentor personas — *Brother*, *Hardcore*, *Strategist*
- ⚠️ Safety layer with risk detection and emergency guidance
- 🚀 Production-ready: Docker, PostgreSQL, HTTPS, CI/CD
